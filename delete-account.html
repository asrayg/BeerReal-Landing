Delete your account in the app

Open BeerReal and go to Profile → Edit Profile → Delete Account, then follow the confirmation steps.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your BeerReal account. Include your username so we can locate and verify your account.

Request account deletion

What will be deleted

  • Your profile and account information
  • Your posts, photos, captions, comments, and reactions
  • Your friend connections, location and venue data, and drink history
  • Your notification and device information

What happens next

We may contact you to verify that you own the account. Once verified, your account and associated data will be permanently deleted. Some information may be retained only when required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes.
