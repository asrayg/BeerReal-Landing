Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Download BeerReal from the App Store, open the app, and sign up with your email. You'll need to verify your email before you can start posting.

Why can't I see my friends' posts?

BeerReal uses a locked feed — you need to post first before you can see what your friends are up to. This way, everyone shares authentically.

How do I add friends?

Go to the search tab, find your friend by username, and send them a friend request. They'll see it in their notifications and can accept from there.

How does drink tracking work?

When you create a post, you can log what you're drinking — beer, cocktail, shot, wine, or other. Your drinks are tracked over time and shown in your profile's contribution grid.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Go to Profile → Edit Profile and scroll to the bottom to find the delete account option. You can also request account deletion on the web.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link.

Why am I getting notifications?

BeerReal sends notifications for friend requests and inactivity reminders. You can manage notification preferences in your device's Settings → BeerReal → Notifications.
