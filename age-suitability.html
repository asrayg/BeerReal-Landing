Age Suitability

Last updated: April 2026

Age Requirement

BeerReal is intended for adults aged 21 and over. The app involves alcohol-related content including drink logging, venue tagging, and social sharing of nightlife experiences.

Why 21+?

BeerReal is designed around the social experience of going out and drinking. All core features — drink tracking, bar crawl planning, and the social feed — are centered on alcohol consumption. We require users to be of legal drinking age in the United States (21+) to use the app.

Content Overview

  • Alcohol references: Users log and share the drinks they consume (beer, cocktails, shots, wine, etc.)
  • User-generated photos: Users post photos of their drinks and selfies from nights out
  • Location sharing: Users share which bars, pubs, and venues they visit
  • Social interaction: Users can view friends' posts, react, and comment

Age Verification

Users must confirm they are 21 or older when creating an account. We reserve the right to remove any account suspected of belonging to a minor.

Parental Guidance

This app is not suitable for children or teenagers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has created an account, please contact us at support@beerreal.app and we will promptly delete the account and all associated data.

Contact

Questions about age suitability? Email us at support@beerreal.app.
